Operating document

Pilot Blueprint

A 2-bed residential memory-care home + clinical backup — the pilot before any scale. Mom is resident zero; that is the quality gate.

1. The model

A residential memory-care home + clinical backup — NOT an on-site hospital. Residential care keeps Aangan in the lightly-regulated senior-care lane. On-site medical is a nursing room to stabilise, not treatment beds. Acute care happens at Al-Falah (5 km, ~10 min); serious/tertiary at Amrita Faridabad (24 km). Treatment beds on-site would trigger the Clinical Establishments Act — avoided by design.

5. Four legal must-haves before any outside resident

  • Doctor of Record — a registered physician responsible for resident care.
  • Liability / professional indemnity insurance — the #1 risk is elder injury/death.
  • Registration — senior citizens' residential/care home under Haryana rules.
  • Signed admission & care agreement with each resident's family (in this pack).

6. Phased sequence

  1. Medical net first — Al-Falah meeting, Amrita pre-registration, ambulance + standby car, nursing room kit. Before any travel.
  2. Site prep — fence the safe garden, hazard-proof rooms, set up the nursing room.
  3. Staff — hire & onboard nurse + attendants early, shadowing family.
  4. Trial run with family present — live the full routine for a few days.
  5. Care file assembled (medical summary, routine, emergency protocol, ID).
  6. Only then — legal must-haves in place → invite one carefully-chosen outside resident.
  7. Review at 3–6 months → decide whether to scale.

The Home operates a small residential memory-care facility providing supervised, non-hospital residential care, including for persons living with dementia. It provides supervision, assistance and coordination of medical care, and does not provide in-patient medical treatment on its premises.

⭐ Golden rule. When in doubt, GO. Al-Falah is 10 minutes away — transfer EARLY, don't "wait and see." If an ambulance will be slow to Dhauj, the standby car may be faster — use it. Always send someone who knows her + the Care File + medicines.

If this happens → do this

🔴 Unconscious / not breathing normally — Call ambulance + Al-Falah NOW. Lay flat, tilt head, check breathing. If trained & no breathing → start CPR. Nothing by mouth. Call family + doctor.
🔴 Chest pain / breathlessness / blue lips — Ambulance + Al-Falah NOW. Sit upright, loosen clothing, calm her. Oxygen if trained. [Aspirin ONLY if doctor pre-authorised]. Don't let her walk.
🔴 Stroke — sudden F.A.S.T. — Face droop · Arm weakness · Speech slurred · Time: call ambulance now, note the time symptoms started. Nothing by mouth. → Al-Falah / Amrita.
🔴 Choking — Can't cough/speak → 5 back blows between shoulder blades, then 5 abdominal thrusts, repeat. If she collapses → CPR + ambulance.
🔴 Seizure — Protect head, clear space, turn on side, do NOT restrain or put anything in mouth. Time it. >5 min or repeated → ambulance. Call doctor.
🔴 Fall — Don't yank her up. Check pain, head injury, deformity, bleeding. If head hit / can't move a limb / severe pain / on blood thinners → don't move her, call ambulance. If clearly minor → help up slowly, watch for hours, log it.
🟠 Missing / wandered off — Search safe zone, house, every room, vehicles, gate, any water body. Note last-seen time + clothing. Call family + neighbours + farm help at once. Search outward toward [nearest road / a place she talks about]. Call police / 112 quickly — do NOT wait hours. Grab her recent photo from the Care File.
🟠 High fever / vomiting / diarrhoea / not passing urine — Call doctor (teleconsult after hours). Sip fluids if conscious. New confusion / drowsiness / dehydration → Al-Falah. Log temperature.
🟠 Won't eat / drink / sudden confusion worse than usual — In dementia this often signals infection, dehydration or pain. Call the doctor the same day. Offer fluids. Drowsy/very confused → Al-Falah.
🟡 Agitation / sundowning — Not a medical emergency. Don't argue or restrain. Lower noise, dim harsh light, redirect: comfort item, familiar song, tea, a garden walk, calm voice. Never force or scold.
🟡 Minor bleeding / cut — Clean, apply pressure, dress. Watch (thin skin / blood thinners). Deep/won't stop → Al-Falah. Log it.

Every call: have ready

Her name, age, condition, allergies · what happened & when it started · the Care File + medicines list · her ID · send the standby car if the ambulance is slow.

Don't

✗ No food/water to an unconscious or choking person · ✗ don't move a bad fall · ✗ no new medicine not prescribed for her · ✗ don't argue with or restrain a confused resident · ✗ don't wait to "see if it improves" for red-flag symptoms — GO.
