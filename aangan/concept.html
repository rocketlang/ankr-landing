ConceptAangan

The courtyard where generations meet.

A place for our elders to spend the day — gardens to walk, music to share, children's laughter, an unhurried lunch, and the plain dignity of feeling at home. Ten minutes from a hospital, held by the hills.

Why Aangan

The courtyard, built again.

Aangan is the courtyard at the centre of an old Indian home — where grandparents sat in the morning sun, where children played at their feet, where the whole day gathered and no one was in a hurry. We are building that courtyard again, on a farm in the Aravallis.

Not a facility at the end of a narrow lane, but a green, open, gentle place where an elder's day is spent well — with company, with things to do at their own pace, and with the quiet respect of being somewhere beautiful. A day club first; a second home when a family needs one.

A day at Aangan

An unhurried rhythm.

Elders arrive after breakfast and are home by dusk. The day moves at their pace — nothing is compulsory, everything is offered.

The days, filled gently

Things to do — at their own pace.

Every activity is memory-friendly, unhurried, and entirely optional. The point is engagement and joy, never a schedule to keep up with.

Aangan & the little ones

Where children keep elders young.

The heart of Aangan is a simple, powerful idea: bring the generations back together. We partner with local schools so children visit to read, sing, garden and celebrate festivals — and the elders who wish become storytellers, teachers, and grandparents-in-residence.

For an elder living with memory loss, a child's visit does what no medicine can — it brings purpose, laughter, and a reason to light up. And the children grow up knowing their elders. It is offered, never obligatory — for those who can, and those who want to.

How it works — gently

  • ◦Opt-in, both sides. Elders choose to join; families and schools consent.
  • ◦Always supervised. Short, joyful, staff-led sessions — reading, songs, planting.
  • ◦At the elder's pace. A wave from a bench counts as much as a story told.
  • ◦Health-aware. Gentle screening so visits are safe for everyone.

The plan

Start as a day club. Grow with care.

Dignity over scale. We begin with the day club — light, joyful, and safe — and add overnight care only as we prove we can do it beautifully.
