The Canonical Formula

K = I × L × R × Rep    V = K × F
K = intrinsic worth at this universe state [1–10,000]  ·  V = deliverable value today = K × current feasibility [1–100,000]
The formula is multiplicative, never additive. That is the whole point — see the Jugaad Elimination Property below.

The Jugaad Elimination Property. Because Rep sits inside K as a multiplier, any architecture needing manual application at scale (Rep = 1–2) sees its K collapse no matter how high its Impact, Leverage, or Reach. A brilliant idea that cannot auto-propagate cannot win. I = L = R = 10, Rep = 2 → K = 2,000   |   Rep = 9 → K = 81,000   — a 40× gap you cannot argue away by summing.

The Guards Layer (v2) — outside the multiplication; they never change the math

VIVECHANA-012
Reversibility Veto
Classify worst case: Reversible / Recoverable / Irreversible. An irreversible option cannot rank #1 on V alone. The number ranks; it never authorizes the unrecoverable.
VIVECHANA-013
Calibration Anchors
Score each axis against the fixed exemplar card above — not by mood. The same card every session is what makes the delta a map.
VIVECHANA-014
Evidence Grade
Tag each axis Measured · Estimated · Guessed. A V resting on a Guess at its highest axis is the first thing to verify before banking.
VIVECHANA-015
Null Baseline
Every ranking includes a scored do-nothing row. "Not yet" must win or lose on the same scale — a scored decision, never a failure.

What Must Never Change

Multiplicative, never additive. The 40× Jugaad gap stands. K and V are always two separate numbers.
Always dated. V is a snapshot of universe state, never a stored permanent truth. Re-run at disruption — the delta is the map.
The veto precedes V. A score never authorizes the unrecoverable. The guards condition the inputs; they never enter the multiplication.
